How to use MnM Assistant

A second-monitor board for Monsters & Memories: spawn timers, camp counters, buffs, notes and reference tools. Everything saves automatically in this browser.

The toolbar
  • ⏱ 5m 6m 8m 10m add a timer in one click. For another length, type it in the box (7:30, 90s, 1h30m) and press Enter.
  • ＋ Add: a Count, Named / PH camp counter, Buff Tracker, Note or Checklist, and notes you've put away.
  • Tools: show or hide the reference panels (below). The number shows how many are open.
  • 🔔 mutes all alerts. ☰ has Tidy panels, save / load layout, reset all, theme, test sound and this guide.
Moving and arranging panels
  • Move: drag a panel by its title bar. It snaps to the grid; a red outline means it would overlap.
  • Resize: drag the bottom-right corner. Timer clocks and counter numbers grow with the panel.
  • Rename: right-click a panel's title. ⚙ opens its settings.
  • Delete: ✕ (Shift + click skips the question). ☰ → Tidy panels packs everything neatly.
Timers
  • Click the clock to start it, and again to restart it when the mob is killed.
  • Right-click the time to set how much is actually left. −1m / +1m nudge it; Pause holds it.
  • When it ends you hear a chime, or the timer's name read aloud if you've named it.
  • Name a timer after the NPC: its kill history and loot are remembered under that name, and earlier NPC names are suggested.
Named / PH camps
  • + PH and + Named log each kill (and restart the linked timer). A named kill asks what dropped; that's optional.
  • Link a counter to a timer by dragging one onto the other (you're asked first).
  • 📜 shows the camp's history: named odds, typical respawn, loot drop rates and the kill log. New session starts the counts over; a kill more than an hour after the last one starts a new session on its own.
  • In the loot list, 💰 checks what a drop sells for and 📖 looks it up on the wiki.
Buffs, notes and checklists
  • Buff Tracker: add buffs with their durations, click one when you cast it, and get a warning before it fades. Click its time to correct it. Save groups of buffs as sets (⚙).
  • Notes: free text with A− / A+. — puts a note away; bring it back from ＋ Add.
  • Checklists: type 18x Copper Ore, or paste a whole list or a wiki materials table, then click items to check them off.
Tools
  • 😈 / 😇 Naughty and Nice lists: check a player's name before you group, and add or move people with a note.
  • 🛡️ Classes: a snapshot of each class, how to play it, race and trait picks, key abilities, leveling path and gear.
  • 💰 Prices: what an item sells for on Trem, what buyers offer and what a vendor pays.
  • 📖 Wiki: search the community wiki and read pages here. 📖 buttons around the app look things up for you.
  • 📊 Level range: the lowest and highest levels a level can group with.
  • 📖 Spells: every spell by class and level, with the cost of a level's spells.
  • 🗺️ Leveling: camps and hunting spots for a level or level range, by zone.
  • 🧭 Map: zone maps from MnM Atlas; scroll over the map to zoom, or find an NPC on any map.
Saving, backups and themes
  • Everything is saved in this browser as you go. Kill histories, loot, players and notes stay even when you delete a panel.
  • ☰ → Save layout to file makes a backup, or lets you move your board to another computer; Load layout brings it back (histories and lists are merged, not replaced).
  • ☰ → Theme: pick a look for your class.
