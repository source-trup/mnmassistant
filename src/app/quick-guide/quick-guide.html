How to use MnM Assistant

A second-monitor companion for Monsters & Memories: the wiki, zone maps, Trem prices, your wishlist and leveling and class guides one click away, plus spawn timers, camp trackers, buffs and notes. Everything saves automatically in this browser.

The tools (top of the screen)

The row of buttons beside the title. Point at one to see what it does; click it to show its panel, and again to hide it. A lit button is open.

  • 📖 Wiki: search the community wiki and read pages right here. 📖 buttons around the app look things up for you.
  • 🧭 Map: zone maps from MnM Atlas; scroll over the map to zoom, or find an NPC on any map.
  • 💰 Price Checker: what an item sells for on Trem, what buyers offer and what a vendor pays.
  • ⭐ Wishlist: items you're after, with their stats, who drops them and wiki links. Add your own note on where something drops when the sites don't know.
  • ⚔️ Gear Planner: put your gear on a paper doll (one per character), then see every upgrade by slot, mob level and source, and the biggest ones around your level.
  • 🗺️ Leveling Spots: pick your class, level and solo or group to see the zones whose mobs con right for you, best first (from Worth the Camp).
  • 🪄 Spells: every spell by class and level, with the cost of a level's spells. Right-click a spell you've bought to leave it out of the totals.
  • 🛡️ Class Guides: a snapshot of each class, how to play it, race and trait picks, key abilities, leveling path and gear.
  • 📊 Group Range: enter a level to see the lowest and highest levels it can group with.
  • 😈 Naughty / 😇 Nice: check a player's name before you group, and add or move people with a note.
The rest of the toolbar
  • ＋ Add: a Timer or a Named / PH timer (6 minutes, or 5m / 8m / 10m, or type any length such as 7:30, 90s, 1h30m), a Session timer that counts up, and a Count, Buff Tracker, Note or Checklist. Notes and Named / PH timers you've put away are listed there too.
  • 🕰️ Game time: the in-game clock, matching the wiki's, with how long until 6 PM and 6 AM. Click it for the date, all the coming hours and spawn windows you add (e.g. a night-only named, 10 PM–4 AM).
  • ? opens this guide. 🔔 mutes all alerts. ☰ has Tidy panels, save / load layout, reset all, theme and test sound.
Moving and arranging panels
  • Move: drag a panel by its title bar. It snaps to the grid; a red outline means it would overlap.
  • Resize: drag the bottom-right corner. Timer clocks and counter numbers grow with the panel.
  • Rename: right-click a panel's title. ⚙ opens its settings.
  • Delete: ✕ (Shift + click skips the question). ☰ → Tidy panels packs everything neatly.
  • Pop out: ⧉ on a timer, Named / PH timer, counter or Buff Tracker moves it into a small window you can keep over the game. In Edge and Chrome it stays on top by itself; in Firefox or Waterfox press Win+Ctrl+T on it (PowerToys Always On Top). A Named / PH timer pops out as a compact panel. ⤓ or closing the window brings it back.
Timers
  • Click the clock to start it, and again to restart it when the mob is killed.
  • Right-click the time to set how much is actually left. −1m / +1m nudge it; Pause holds it.
  • When it ends you hear a chime, or the timer's name read aloud if you've named it.
Session timers
  • A timer that counts up (＋ Add → Session timer). Click the clock to start or pause; End session finishes the session and keeps it.
  • ＋ Counter adds counters to it, or drag a counter panel onto it (it asks first). Right-click a counter's number to type it, or its name to rename it.
  • 📊 shows each session's counts with a chart, and all-time totals, rates and a bar per session.
Named / PH timers
  • One panel for a camp: the respawn countdown on top, placeholder and named kills below. + PH and + Named log the kill and restart the countdown. A named kill asks what dropped (with suggestions as you type); that's optional.
  • Name it after the named NPC (right-click the title): its kill history and loot are kept under that name, earlier NPC names are suggested, and it says "Grimfang is up".
  • After a few kills it shows the camp's estimated respawn: worked out from the whole kill log, so late presses, missed spawns and breaks don't throw it off.
  • 📜 shows the camp's history: named odds, estimated respawn, loot drop rates and the kill log. New session starts the counts over; a kill more than an hour after the last one starts a new session on its own.
  • — puts it away (counts and history kept); bring it back from ＋ Add. In the loot list, 💰 checks what a drop sells for and 📖 looks it up on the wiki.
Buffs, notes and checklists
  • Buff Tracker: add buffs with their durations, click one when you cast it, and get a warning before it fades. Click its time to correct it. Save groups of buffs as sets (⚙).
  • Notes: free text with A− / A+. — puts a note away; bring it back from ＋ Add.
  • Checklists: type 18x Copper Ore, or paste a whole list or a wiki materials table, then click items to check them off. 💰 shows what each item costs on Trem and the total still to buy.
Saving, backups and themes
  • Everything is saved in this browser as you go. Kill histories, loot, players, your wishlist and notes stay even when you delete a panel.
  • ☰ → Save layout to file makes a backup, or lets you move your board to another computer; Load layout brings it back (histories and lists are merged, not replaced).
  • ☰ → Theme: pick a look for your class.
  • The site counts page views and which tools get opened, anonymously and without cookies. Nothing you type or save is sent. ☰ → Share anonymous usage stats turns it off.
