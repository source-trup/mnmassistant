Buff sets

Times accept 36:00, 1:30:00, 90s, 1h30m or plain minutes. Buffs you name are spoken (“… is fading”); ones with a generated name like “35:00 buff” just chime. Buff sets save the names and timings, so you can load your usual buffs into any Buff Tracker; they're also included in saved layout files.
